Radio guide

Tune the station's frequency in SRS (AM), press your push-to-talk, wait half a second, and talk like you would to a real controller. Keep each call to one request.

The order is the same everywhere: who you're calling, who you are, what you want. "Overlord, Kupi 3-1, picture." The station knows who you are from your SRS name, so pick a name like your callsign.

Cold jet? The radios need electrical power before SRS can transmit. Tune exactly to the frequency, for example 251.500, not 251.5 on a different preset.

If a station doesn't answer

  • Check you're on the right frequency and AM.
  • Wait half a second after pressing push-to-talk. SRS clips the first moment of every transmission, and a clipped station name is the most common miss.
  • Say it again, shorter. "Tower, Lama 1-1, request taxi" works better than one long sentence.
251.000

Overlord

The GCI controller: an older, calm voice with the whole picture. Overlord shares the frequency with your group, so it answers calls with its name, or with a clear GCI request like "picture" or "bogey dope" if the start of the call got clipped.

You sayOverlord answers
Overlord, Kupi 3-1, bogey dope.Kupi 3-1, Overlord, BRAA, two-seven-zero, 25, 15 thousand, hot, single contact, Fishbed.
Overlord, Kupi 3-1, picture.All groups in range, nearest first, with BRAA, altitude, aspect and count.
Overlord, Kupi 3-1, ground threats.The three nearest hostile ground groups by bearing and range, then "stand by to copy coordinates" for the nearest.
Overlord, Kupi 3-1, request coordinates two.Coordinates for the second threat on that list.
Overlord, Kupi 3-1, request tanker.The nearest friendly tanker that suits your jet, with bearing, range, angels, TACAN and frequency (see Tankers).
Overlord, Kupi 3-1, say friendlies.Where the other friendly flights are, by bearing, range and altitude.
Overlord, Kupi 3-1, threat warnings off. (or on)Stops or restarts the automatic warnings, for you only.

Warnings you get without asking

  • Air threats when a hostile group comes inside 25 miles, and again inside 10. Groups flying away from you are left out.
  • SAM and triple-A about 3 miles before you enter a site's range: "SAM threat, SA-6, zero-eight-five, 16, approaching its range." Around 30 types are known, from SA-2 and SA-10 down to Shilkas and MANPADS.

Coordinates you can write down

After "stand by to copy" there's a five-second pause, then the coordinates are read once, digit by digit, with a pause between groups. Jets get latitude and longitude in degrees and decimal minutes; the Apache, A-10 and helicopters get an MGRS grid.

At the same time the coordinates appear as a block on your screen, a marker appears on your flight's F10 map, and the target is posted to Discord.

Enemy aircraft are always called by their reporting names: Fishbed, Fulcrum, Flanker, Frogfoot, Hind, Hip. "Threat" on its own means ground threats; ask for bogey dope or picture for air.

Tankers

Ask Overlord for gas. It finds every friendly tanker in the air, on any map, and gives you the nearest one your jet can use: a boom tanker for the Viper, Strike Eagle and Hog, a basket tanker for the Hornet, Tomcat, Harrier and Mirage.

You sayOverlord answers
Overlord, Lama 1-1, request tanker.Lama 1-1, Overlord, Arco 1-1, KC-135, basket, bearing one-niner-four, 25 miles, angels 15, TACAN 52 X-ray, frequency two six one decimal zero.
Overlord, Lama 1-1, request tankers.Up to three tankers, nearest first, each with boom or basket, bearing, range, angels and TACAN.
Overlord, Lama 1-1, where's Texaco?Questions in your own words work too.

If only the wrong kind of tanker is up, Overlord says so and still gives you the nearest one. The frequency and TACAN come from the mission file. If a mission script sets them while the mission runs, Overlord leaves out what it can't see.

For now you still join the tanker the DCS way, with the radio menu or by flying into position. A voice boom operator on the tanker's own frequency is coming next.

Tower

One station is ground and tower for every airfield on every map: Caucasus, Syria, Nevada, the Persian Gulf, Marianas and the rest. Say the airfield's name and Tower answers as that airfield. Say just "Tower" and it answers for the airfield you last talked to, or the one you're nearest. Tower answers every call on its frequency, so the station name is optional.

You sayTower answers
Kutaisi Tower, Lama 1-1, request start-up.Start-up approved, runway in use, wind, altimeter, as Kutaisi Tower.
Tower, Lama 1-1, request information.The airfield's ATIS: information letter, runway, wind, visibility, clouds, temperature, altimeter.
Tower, Lama 1-1, request taxi.Taxi to holding point runway three three, wind, altimeter, hold short runway three three.
Lama 1-1, ready for takeoff.Cleared for takeoff, or hold short with the reason (traffic on the runway or on final).
Tower, Lama 1-1, inbound for landing, with information Bravo.Runway, wind, altimeter and your place in the landing sequence.
Tower, Lama 1-1, request PAR.A precision approach talk-down (see below).
Tower, Lama 1-1, request traffic.Nearby traffic as clock positions: "two o'clock, three miles, four thousand five hundred, Hornet."

Calls Tower makes on its own

  • Hold position if you roll onto the runway without a takeoff clearance.
  • Airborne, contact Overlord after a cleared takeoff.
  • Go around if you're inside about a mile on final while someone is on the runway.
  • Welcome, exit the runway after you land.
  • Traffic alerts when another aircraft is closing on you. Your own flight and aircraft flying formation with you are left out.

Precision approach (PAR)

Tower vectors you onto the extended centreline if you aren't lined up, then talks you down every few seconds: "four miles from touchdown, slightly right of course, turn left heading three-two-niner, on glidepath." Around three miles out it clears you to land if the runway is clear, and ends with "over landing threshold, take over visually." Say "cancel PAR" to stop.

On request

ATIS

The airfield weather, built from the live mission: information letter, zulu time, runway in use, wind, visibility, clouds, temperature and altimeter. The letter changes every hour and whenever the runway in use changes.

Ask Tower for it before you call in: "Tower, Lama 1-1, request information." Then report the letter: "inbound for landing, with information Golf." If yours is out of date, Tower tells you the current one.

A server can also give an airfield its own tower and ATIS frequency on the app's Stations page. That ATIS broadcasts on a loop while someone is tuned in.

Buddy

A relaxed squadron mate with a joke ready, who knows the mission and has read the manuals. Buddy goes quiet on the jokes when you sound busy.

You sayBuddy answers
Buddy, Lama 1-1, how do I set up a JDAM?Three or four steps from your aircraft's manual, then "say next for the rest."
Next.The next steps.
Buddy, what's our task?The mission briefing, time and weather.
Buddy, nearest airfields?Bearing, range, owner, runway in use and tower frequency.
Buddy, where's the nearest bandit?The same live picture Overlord has, in a friendlier voice.

Teach Buddy your aircraft

Put PDF manuals and your own cheat sheets (.txt or .md) in the docs folder, one folder per aircraft: docs\F-18, docs\Apache, docs\General. Buddy searches the folder for the aircraft you're flying and builds the steps from what it finds, using the manual's own switch and page names. If something isn't in the manuals, it says so before giving its best guess.

Paddles

The Case I landing trainer: it watches you from the initial to the wire, coaches each point of the pattern, makes the groove calls of a Landing Signal Officer, and grades the pass.

Where you arePaddles says

DownwindYou're wide at 1.7 miles, move in, come down to six hundred.

Ask "Paddles, how was my pass?" for a debrief with one concrete fix per error. "Paddles, say BRC" gives the ship's heading, final bearing and wind over the deck.

Coaching, not the lens. Glideslope calls are calculated from your position, so fly the ball and treat Paddles as an instructor. Fast and slow calls need an angle-of-attack reference and currently cover the Hornet.
Extras

Map and Discord

Everything a station says to you also appears as a short subtitle on your screen, for you only. Coordinates stay up longer.

The live map shows the mission on any tablet or phone, and the targets Overlord reads out. Two Discord channels can receive posts: one for targets and one for the greenie board.
